Sowjanya Case: ASP Venkatesh Prasanna Requests Govt to Opt Out of SIT

ASP Venkatesh Prasanna requested the government to remove him from the SIT probing the Sowjanya rape and murder case.

Read in: ಕನ್ನಡ

BENGALURU: Bengaluru Rural Additional Superintendent of Police (ASP) Venkatesh Prasanna has written a letter to the Karnataka government requesting to be recused from the Special Investigation Team (SIT) constituted to re-investigate the Sowjanya rape and murder case.

The Supreme Court, while hearing a special leave petition filed by Sowjanya's mother Kusumavathi Gowda, had directed the state government to form an SIT and conduct a fresh probe into the case. The apex court had also instructed the government to complete the investigation and submit a detailed report within three months.

Following the court order, the government appointed IGP N. Shashi Kumar as the head of the SIT. Mangaluru City Police Commissioner C.H. Sudheer Kumar Reddy, State Intelligence SP Nisha James, CID SP Lokesh Jagalasar, and Bengaluru Rural ASP Venkatesh Prasanna were inducted as team members.

However, social activists and Sowjanya's family members raised objections regarding the inclusion of certain officers in the SIT, alleging past corruption charges against them. They specifically demanded the exclusion of Venkatesh Prasanna from the panel. Following these public objections, Venkatesh Prasanna formally petitioned the state government to be removed from the investigation team.

Previously, IPS officer Soumyalatha had also opted out of a separate SIT formed by the state government to investigate alleged criminal activities around the Netravati River under Dharmasthala Police Station limits. She had submitted a letter to SIT chief Pranav Mohanty stating that she was stepping aside due to personal reasons.
